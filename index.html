01 What is Build Day

A whole week to make a game that’s yours.

Roblox is full of players who never stopped to ask how those games got made. Build Day is the week we answer that. You get a creative theme, a real deadline, and Roblox Studio — the free tool behind every experience you already play.

One theme, five days

Every Monday a brand-new creative challenge drops. It’s the only rule: your game has to fit the theme. A surprise twist lands mid-week so you have to keep building.

Mon → Fri

You learn Studio for real

Place and move parts, write scripts in Luau, shape terrain, build a UI, then publish a place other people can actually play. By Friday you actually know Studio.

Build · Script · Publish

It doesn’t have to be perfect

Grey boxes welcome. Default textures absolutely welcome. The reward for Build Day isn’t a trophy — it’s the skills you keep afterwards.

02 How it works

Monday to Friday, here’s the whole week.

Build in your own time — every day opens with a 15-minute live kickoff, and everything is recorded if you miss it. Tap a day to see what you’ll be doing.

Made in Roblox Studio

The same free tool behind every game you already play. Download it once and you can build all week — then keep building long after Build Day ends.

Save my spot

So why would I do this if I only play Roblox?

01

Because you want a challenge

A theme you didn’t pick, five days on the clock, and something to prove. Player or builder — there is no participation trophy in Build Day, so go try to win it.

02

Because you want to build with other people

Team up with friends, or tell us you’re solo and we’ll match you with other players who signed up. Build nights, feedback threads, and someone to scream at when the script breaks.

Teams are optional. Solo entries get the exact same theme, the same week and the same showcase slot.

03 Sign up

Save your spot. It takes a minute.

Free, open to first-timers, and you can switch teams later. Sign up and you’ll get the theme email, the starter files, and a place in a build room full of people doing the exact same thing.

  • ✓ The Monday theme plus a build-plan template
  • ✓ Starter Studio files so you never start from zero
  • ✓ A build room with other players and daily check-ins
  • ✓ A showcase slot on Friday night
  • ✓ Skills that stay with you after the week ends
